# Distances

Astronomy uses different units at different scales, because one unit cannot
usefully span both a planetary orbit and a galaxy.

| Unit | Roughly | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Astronomical unit (AU) | 149.6 million km | distances inside a planetary system |
| Light year | 9.46 trillion km | distances between stars |
| Parsec | 3.26 light years | professional astronomy |

[[Milky Way/Sun/Earth]] sits at 1 AU by definition - the unit was chosen to make
that true.
