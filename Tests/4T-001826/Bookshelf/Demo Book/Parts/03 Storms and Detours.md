# 03 — Storms and Detours

Every honest account has a middle in which very little goes to plan. Three weeks of it, in this case, and a coastline nobody had intended to see.

## Reading order, not folder order

This chapter also sits in `Parts`, right next to [[02 The Harbour]] — yet in the book it stands one level higher, after chapter 01 and its sub-chapter. Neighbours on disk, strangers in the outline. That separation is the point of the model.

The reading order is depth-first: a chapter comes before its sub-chapters, and only then do its siblings follow. Walking this book therefore gives 01, 02, 03, 04.

## Try it

- Drag this row onto the middle of [[04 Homecoming]]. It becomes a sub-chapter of the ending, which reads oddly but works. Drag it back to the lower third of chapter 01 to restore the order.
- Keep pressing `Ctrl+Alt+Page Up` past the first chapter. There is no wrap-around; the status line simply says the start of the book has been reached.
