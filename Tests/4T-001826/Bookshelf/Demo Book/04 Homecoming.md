# 04 — Homecoming

The last chapter, and back in the book folder itself — no subfolder, no ceremony. Arrivals are quieter than departures and take up less room on the page.

## The end of the reading path

Press `Ctrl+Alt+Page Down` here and nothing happens except a note in the status line: the end of the book is reached. The forward button in the panel header is greyed out for the same reason. A silent jump back to chapter 01 would leave you wondering whether the command arrived at all.

## What is left over

One file in this folder belongs to no chapter: [[Notes to Self]]. It is not hidden, it is not lost, and it is not a chapter either — it waits in the **Not linked in** section at the bottom of the panel. Link it in from its context menu and it becomes chapter 05; unlink it again and it drops back down.

## Try it

- Right-click any chapter and choose **New chapter**. Type a name. A file is created next to its parent chapter and linked in immediately.
- Close the file of a chapter, move it outside the app, and reopen the book. The entry stays, marked as missing, and offers to be reassigned.
