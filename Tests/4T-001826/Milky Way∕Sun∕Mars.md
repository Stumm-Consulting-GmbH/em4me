# Mars

The fourth planet from the Sun, and a sibling subpage of
[[Milky Way/Sun/Earth]] - both hang under the same star.

Note the link form: a sibling is reached by its full path, because the short
`[[/Name]]` form always means a subpage of the *current* page.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Distance from the Sun | 1.52 AU |
| Orbital period | 687 days |
| Natural satellites | 2 |

Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos, but they are left out here on purpose:
the hierarchy is meant to be walked, not to be complete.
