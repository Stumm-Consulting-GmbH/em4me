# Light Speed

Light travels 299,792,458 metres per second in a vacuum. That number is not
measured but fixed by definition - the metre is derived from it.

## Travel times

| From | To | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | Earth | about 8 minutes 20 seconds |
| Moon | Earth | about 1.3 seconds |
| Proxima Centauri | Earth | about 4.25 years |

Because nothing outruns light, every one of these times is also a look into the
past: the Sun you see is the Sun of eight minutes ago.
