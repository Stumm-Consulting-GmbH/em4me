# Milky Way

A barred spiral galaxy, and the one we live in. It holds somewhere between 100
and 400 billion stars, and light needs about 100,000 years to cross it.

This page has subpages. Each star below is a subpage of this galaxy, each planet
a subpage of its star, and each moon a subpage of its planet. Open the
**Subpages** panel in the sidebar to walk down the tree, or use the breadcrumb
above a subpage to walk back up.
