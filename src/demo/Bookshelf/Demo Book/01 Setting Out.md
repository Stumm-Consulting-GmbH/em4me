# 01 — Setting Out

The crates were packed three days before anyone admitted the journey was real. That is the way of departures: the work happens long before the moment, and the moment itself is over in a breath.

Nothing here needs the app to be read. This file is plain Markdown, and it will still be plain Markdown when the book is long forgotten.

## What a chapter is

A chapter is a file. That is the whole story. It sits in the book folder, or in a subfolder, and a line in `Book_Settings.mdda` says where it belongs in the reading order. Move the file to another folder and the chapter keeps its place in the outline; move it in the outline and the file stays where it is.

This chapter has one sub-chapter, [[02 The Harbour]], and that one lives in the `Parts` folder. The nesting is declared, not derived from the folder.

## Try it

- Press `Ctrl+Alt+Page Down` to walk on. You will land in a different folder without noticing.
- In the panel, focus this row and press `Alt+↓`. This chapter and its sub-chapter travel together.
- Press `Alt+↑` to put it back.
